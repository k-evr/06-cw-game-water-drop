# 💧 Water Drop Collector · charity: water

A browser-based casual game inspired by the mission of [charity: water](https://www.charitywater.org): bringing clean, safe drinking water to people in developing countries.

**[▶ Play Now](https://k-evr.github.io/CharityWater-Water-Drop-Game/)**

---

## 🎮 How to Play

Water drops fall across the screen. Click (or tap) them to collect clean water and earn points. Watch out for pollutants, as hitting one costs you points **and** a life.

Reach **100 points** before losing all 3 lives to win!

| Object | Action | Effect |
|--------|--------|--------|
| 💧 Water Drop | Click/tap it | +10 points |
| ☠️ Pollutant | Click/tap it | −15 points, −1 life |

---

## ⚙️ Difficulty Modes

| Mode | Drop Speed | Speed Ramp | Points/Drop |
|------|-----------|------------|-------------|
| 💚 Easy | Slow | Every 10 pts | +10 |
| 💙 Normal | Medium | Faster ramp | +10 |
| ❤️ Hard | Fast | Aggressive ramp | +10 |

The game speeds up as you play, so stay sharp!

---

## 🏆 Features

- **Three difficulty levels** — suitable for all ages
- **Score tracking** — your best score per difficulty is saved locally
- **Live stats panel** — tracks drops collected, missed, and current speed multiplier
- **Goal progress bar** — visual feedback toward the 100-point target
- **Mobile-friendly** — fully playable on touch devices
- **Charity mission messaging** — links to donate and learn more about charity: water
